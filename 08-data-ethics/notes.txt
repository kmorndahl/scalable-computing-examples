FAST (ethics in AI):
- fairness
    - data
    - design
    - outcome
    - implementation
- accountability
    - AI are not moral decision makers, need humans
- sustainability
    - safe, accurate, reliable, secure, and robust AI systems
- transparency
    - avoid a ‘mathematical glass box’ where the code and mathematics behind the algorithm are openly available, but there is a lack of rationale about how or why the model goes from input to output
